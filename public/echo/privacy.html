Echo privacy policy

Echo is the meeting notes tool of Ecultify, used by Ecultify's own team through its task tracker. Updated 13 November 2026.

What Echo records

When a team member who has added Echo to their browser is in a Google Meet call, Echo records the call's audio and that person's microphone. A notice on the meeting page shows that it is recording and has a Stop button, and recording can be switched off in Echo's settings. While the person is muted in Meet, their microphone is not recorded. The team member is expected to tell the other people in the meeting that it is being recorded for notes.

What it stores

  • The meeting title, the names shown in the meeting, the audio recording, the transcript, and the notes and action items written from it.
  • These are stored on Ecultify's own servers and shown to the team member who recorded the meeting in their task tracker account. Notes filed under a client brand are also visible to that brand's managers at Ecultify.
  • The extension itself keeps only its settings and the team member's link to their task tracker account in the browser's local storage.

Google Calendar

A team member can choose to connect their Google Calendar. Echo then has read-only access to their calendar events and uses it for one thing: to find the invite that matches a meeting it recorded, and read that invite's title and guest names and email addresses, so the notes show who was in the meeting. Echo does not change calendars, does not read other events for any other purpose, and does not share this data with anyone outside Ecultify. The connection can be removed at any time from the task tracker profile page or from the Google Account's security settings.

Echo's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who processes it

  • Meta's model API transcribes the audio and writes the notes.
  • Google delivers summary emails, and only when someone at Ecultify chooses to send them.

Echo does not sell data, show ads, or use recordings for anything other than producing the notes for that meeting.

Keeping and deleting

Recordings, transcripts and notes are kept so the team can refer back to them. Anyone who was in a recorded meeting can ask for it to be deleted.
